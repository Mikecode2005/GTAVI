# Editorial checklist

Use this checklist before adding a GTA VI fact to the site:

- [ ] Is the claim published by Rockstar Games or Take-Two?
- [ ] Is the source URL direct and still accessible?
- [ ] Is the claim current, or does it describe a superseded announcement?
- [ ] Have confirmed fact, interpretation, and rumor been separated?
- [ ] Does the copy avoid presenting an unannounced PC, online, map, or mechanic detail as fact?
- [ ] Does the change preserve the fan-made and unaffiliated disclaimer?

If the answer to the first question is no, keep the claim in the unknown/rumor category or leave it out.
